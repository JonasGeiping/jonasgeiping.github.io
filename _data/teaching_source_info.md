## AI Safety Course

- Title: AI Safety (no module number since it's a new course)
- Lecturers: Maksym Andriushchenko, Sahar Abdelnabi, Jonas Geiping
- Time of lecture: Any time between 10am and 6pm from Monday to Thursday
- Preferred lecture hall/room: Lecture halls in Maria-von-Linden Str. (preferably Maria-von-Linden Str. 1) for lectures and exercises.
- Expected number of participants: 100 students.
- Teaching assignments: Remunerated either through the Tübingen AI center (we will discuss this with the center) or through our own budgets at the ELLIS Institute Tübingen
- Credits: 6 credit points
- Format: 1 lecture and 1 exercise session per week
- Target: Students (primarily at the master's level) who are interested in understanding and improving modern AI models.
- Prerequisites: No formal course prerequisites, although prior coursework in deep learning, statistical machine learning, or LLMs would be very helpful.
- Grading: Most likely 50% from a final project and 50% from a closed-book exam.
- ALMA Link: https://alma.uni-tuebingen.de:443/alma/pages/startFlow.xhtml?_flowId=detailView-flow&unitId=98925&periodId=229&navigationPosition=studiesOffered,searchCourses

Course description:
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to safety and reliability in modern AI systems, with a focus on large language models and AI agents. Students will explore technical vulnerabilities including adversarial robustness, jailbreaks, prompt injections, and hallucinations, while examining approaches to detect and prevent these failures. The curriculum covers alignment challenges such as emergent misalignment, scalable oversight, and AI control methods for managing increasingly capable systems. Students will gain hands-on experience with interpretability techniques, evaluation methods, and practical tools for watermarking, detecting AI-generated content, and understanding copyright implications in LLMs. By the end of the course, students will understand both the theoretical foundations and practical aspects of building safer AI systems, including methods for predicting AI capabilities.
